Same Day, Two Systems

What the difference looks like.

The Scenario

Two athletes on the same soccer team. The squad flies across three time zones, then plays a match the next afternoon in 96°F heat and high humidity.

Athlete 1 · With LIV.U
Livy
What the system knew
Her cardiovascular baseline and genetic profile, the three-time-zone travel, the match timing, and the heat in the forecast — all read silently, in advance.
What she saw that morning
"Body clock still catching up from travel. Heat will add real cardiovascular load today — hydrate early, shorten your warm-up, and ease into the first twenty minutes."
Why it was personal
Her genetic profile flags a slower heat-adaptation response, so the guidance leaned harder on hydration and pacing than it would for a teammate.
The result
She arrived adapted, paced the opening, and still had something left when the match was decided late.
Athlete 2 · With a basic tracker
Patricia
What the system knew
Heart rate and step count. Nothing about the travel, the match, or the heat.
What she saw that morning
"78 bpm · 2,400 steps." A number, with no context and no guidance.
Why it wasn't personal
The same readout every athlete gets, on every day, in every climate — blind to who she is and what she's about to face.
The result
She trained as if it were a normal day at home, pushed too hard early in the heat, and faded before the match was decided.

Same flight. Same heat. Same match.
One system understood all three — the other counted steps.

Why Us

Most wearables track what your body did.
We measure how your body adapts.

CardioGen Intelligence OS is a continuous cardiovascular and genetic intelligence system that maps how each athlete responds to training, stress, and recovery in real time — individually, precisely, and biologically.

While legacy wearables summarize sleep and recovery into simplified scores, we model the underlying physiologic engine driving performance: cardiovascular response, autonomic stress load, and adaptive capacity.

This is not wellness tracking. This is performance intelligence.

From the Founder

"For decades, athlete monitoring has focused on output — speed, workload, sleep, recovery. We believe the next frontier is understanding the biology underneath it.

Emerging research in epigenetics and elite sport is beginning to show that genetics and biologic response patterns may help explain why some athletes recover differently, adapt differently, and carry different injury risk profiles under the same physical load. LIV.U was built around that future: combining cardiovascular intelligence, physiologic monitoring, and human performance data to help athletes better understand how their bodies respond to stress, training, and recovery."

— BRIAN LENTZ, MPAS, PA-C · UNIVERSITY OF PENNSYLVANIA CARDIOLOGY
